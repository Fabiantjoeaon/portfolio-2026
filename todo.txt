POSSIBLE UPGRADES:
Roses: replace the vertex-animation texture with Vertex Animation Video. Today a 3 MB float EXR is decoded on the CPU. The replacement streams a smaller, exact track and keeps live material response. It's experimental, needs a Blender re-bake with the Pro exporter, and MeadowTracking's stem-tip lookup would need reworking.
Pointer-reactive 2D smoke (three-blocks/smoke). It could replace or layer on PointerFeedbackMap's flow in the Cube scene, or add distortion over the project screen. See their smoke post-processing example.
Film grain (filmHD). Your grain.js is exported but unused, and currently only dithering runs. filmHD is the ready-made version if you want that look.
About portrait as Gaussian splats. This is 0.14's headline feature and would replace the 100k-sprite point cloud with a captured splat. It's a different aesthetic and needs the Pro capture pipeline, so treat it as an idea rather than an upgrade.
